UK Motorsport Cluster

The UK Motorsport Cluster is a hub of precision engineering, design, and advanced manufacturing. It brings together R&D, production, logistics, and commercial services — fuelling global motorsport and high-performance industries.

41,000

The region supports over 4,300 motorsport-related companies and employs 41,000 people.

£16bn

Motorsport and engineering services contributes £16bn to the UK economy.

The drive to innovate

Competition is a powerful engine for innovation, and motorsport exemplifies this in the drive to outpace rivals by creating cars and processes that are better, faster, and more efficient. This fuels continual learning and experimentation.

R&D Investment

F1 reinvests 30% of sales into R&D, far above most industries, driving fast paced innovation.

Accelerate Ahead

New teams in F1

Entry of new teams to F1 reinforces UK’s pivotal role in motorsport engineering. Audi and Cadillac have both chosen the UK Motorsport Cluster for their UK base, and Ford is partnering with Red Bull who are already based within the cluster.

Integrated Innovation Ecosystem

The UK Racing Cluster is a global hub of motorsport innovation, strengthened by new entrants setting up in the region. It emerged post-WWII, combining aerospace expertise and the Midlands’ rich automotive heritage. In the 1950s, firms like Cooper and Lotus drove British dominance in motorsport, especially Formula One.

Anchored by world-class research and testing facilities

Silverstone Circuit

Silverstone

Silverstone is a 5.891 km UK circuit and home to the British Grand Prix, featuring F1’s largest pit complex and advanced facilities for race engineering and R&D.

Bristol & Bath Science Park

South Gloucestershire

The park hosts IAAPS and a cluster of mobility-tech SMEs developing next-gen vehicle systems. Companies in software and engineering also benefit from IAAPS’s advanced facilities and expertise.

Bicester Motion

Oxfordshire

Bicester Motion is a 444-acre mobility hub, focused on automotive and aviation. Once RAF Bicester, it now hosts specialist firms spanning engineering, prototyping and restoration. NEOM McLaren Electric Racing, will be joined by the Audi F1 Project with Sauber Motorsport, and YASA’s HQ.

MIRA Technology Park

Warwickshire

MIRA Technology Parkis Europe’s leading automotive and mobility R&D campus, combining specialist engineering expertise, 38+ advanced laboratories and world-class proving grounds. Home to over 35 businesses, it accelerates product development across automotive, clean technology, AI and next-generation mobility in a collaborative innovation cluster.

National Automotive Innovation Centre

Warwick University, Coventry

The National Automotive Innovation Centre at Warwick University is a collaborative hub where industry and academic experts work together to research, design, and develop advanced vehicle technologies and future mobility solutions.

Bicester Motion

Oxfordshire

Bicester Motion is a 444-acre mobility hub in Motorsport Valley, focused on automotive and aviation. Once RAF Bicester, it now hosts 50+ specialist firms spanning engineering, prototyping and restoration. NEOM McLaren Electric Racing, be joined by the Audi F1 Project with Sauber Motorsport, and YASA’s 88,000sq ft HQ.

Institute for Advanced Automotive Propulsion Systems

Bristol & Bath Science Park, South Gloucestershire

Part of the University of Bath, IAAPS accelerates clean powertrain R&D with advanced prototyping and composites expertise for next-gen automotive tech.

Silverstone Technology Park

Northamptonshire

Silverstone Park is a 131-acre tech and motorsport hub next to Silverstone Circuit, home to 60+ firms and expanding to 250 by 2030. It offers specialist facilities like the Hexagon Metrology Centre, Digital Manufacturing Centre, Applus+ 3C Test lab, and the National College for Motorsport.

UK Battery Industrialisation Centre (UKBIC)

Coventry

UKBIC is a £200M open-access facility bridging battery R&D and production. It offers pilot lines, test labs, and system integration to help scale technologies from prototype to mass manufacturing.

Faraday Institution

Harwell Campus, Oxfordshire

The Faraday Institute leads UK battery research, advancing next-gen technologies with 500+ researchers and strong industry ties, including motorsport.

Diamond Light Source

Harwell Campus, Oxfordshire

The Diamond Light Source is the UK’s national synchrotron facility, producing ultra-bright light to study materials at the atomic level. It supports automotive research on emissions, fuel efficiency, and advanced materials, accelerating innovation in lightweight parts, electric drivetrains, and clean combustion.

UTAC Millbrook

Bedfordshire

UTAC Millbrook in Bedfordshire is a leading 700-acre vehicle testing facility with diverse tracks and advanced labs for emissions, safety, and autonomous systems. It supports everything from passenger cars to military vehicles in a secure, innovation-focused environment.

National Automotive Innovation Centre

UK universities offer motorsport-specific degrees; major hubs across Motorsport Cluster include Cranfield, Warwick, Bath, and Oxford Brookes.

Talent Pipeline

Supported by over 200 colleges and strong industry links with F1 and Formula E teams through initiatives like Formula Student and Formula Student AI.

Cranfield’s airport facility provides unique R&D collaboration opportunities with industry.

More than a race

Formula 1 is more than speed — it’s a catalyst for breakthrough innovation. Across the innovative landscape, advances are no longer discrete, and the breadth of the motorsport industry amplifies its influence across industries and global challenges.

Sustainable technologies

Hybrid/electric vehicles, 100% sustainable fuel in F1 by 2026, aerodynamic efficiency and lightweight materials.

Safety

Innovations like HANS, SAFER barriers, crash structures now used in daily life and public infrastructure.

Autonomous driving

Competitions and partnerships have positioned the UK as a global AV R&D centre.

Smart manufacturing

AI/ML, digital twins, and advanced simulations are applied beyond motorsport—into healthcare, aerospace and logistics.

Public mobility

Tech from race telemetry informs metro and air traffic systems, as well as extending into sectors from med tech to renewables.

Motorsport delivers strategic value for priority sectors

Motorsport may seem distant from everyday life, but its impact is real and far-reaching. The UK Motorsport Cluster is a global R&D powerhouse — a national success story whose full economic potential deserves recognition and support.
